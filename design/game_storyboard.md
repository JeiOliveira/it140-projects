## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

Lockdown takes place during an underground music event where a sudden blackout causes the venue to enter lockdown. The game mixes survival, mystery, and a small cybersecurity element as the player tries to regain control of the building and escape.

**Storyline:**

During a late night underground music event, the power suddenly goes out, there is no cell service, and the entire venue is on lockdown. Someone inside has taken control of the building's security system, disabling cameras and locking the exits. The player's goal is to explore the venue and collect six items that will help them survive, uncover who caused the lockdown, and escape. The player must collect every item before entering the Control Room and encountering The Operator, the person responsible for taking over the venue.

8. Control Room - Villain Room

## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items.
Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. Flashlight — Bar
2. VIP Wristband — VIP Lounge
3. Master Key — Backstage
4. Walkie-Talkie — DJ Booth
5. Security Keycard — Security Office
6. USB Drive — Back Office

If you add rooms beyond the minimum, add an item for every additional room
except the start room and villain room.

## Villain

The Operator: A mysterious person who has taken control of the venue's security system and placed the entire building into lockdown. The Operator is hiding in the Control Room and is using the venue's cameras and electronic locks to prevent anyone from escaping.
